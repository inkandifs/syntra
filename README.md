SYNTRA

One System. Every Case. Connected.

An AI-powered Unified Complaint Intelligence & Cross-Bureau Coordination Platform.

Built by Team Outlaw for Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26264

The Problem

What happens when multiple departments investigate the same institutional complaint without knowing it?

Duplicated efforts. Delayed resolutions. Disconnected decisions.

Our Solution

SYNTRA transforms fragmented complaint handling into one intelligent, coordinated workflow.

Key Innovations

- SYRA AI | An action-oriented assistant that goes beyond answering questions.
- Overlap Intelligence | Identifies potentially related complaints across bureaus.
- CaseGraph | Interactive visualization of connected cases and institutions.
- Coordination Rooms | Shared workspaces for interdepartmental collaboration.
- DelayRadar | Deadline monitoring and escalation management.
- Decision Trail | Transparent tracking of case actions and decisions.
- Smart Analytics | Real-time dashboards, insights and reports.

Our Vision

From isolated complaints to connected intelligence.

Detect smarter. Coordinate faster. Resolve better.
